Scans all account devices, including excluded devices. Enter the subnet your Tuya devices are on, such as your IoT VLAN. Several can be separated by commas. Finds each device's local IP and protocol version by connecting to it on TCP port 6668 with its local key. Devices on another VLAN work too, as long as your firewall lets this machine reach port 6668 there. Up to 1,024 addresses. If you have many devices, the scan may take longer.
