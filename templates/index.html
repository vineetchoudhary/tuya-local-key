Tuya Local Key

Log in

Where to find your user code
  1. Open the Smart Life app on your phone
  2. Tap Me in the bottom-right
  3. Tap the ⚙️ Settings gear in the top-right
  4. Tap Account and Security
  5. Scroll to the bottom and copy your User Code, then paste it above

Scan to confirm

How to scan the QR code
  1. Open the Smart Life app on your phone
  2. Tap the ➕ in the top-right, then tap Scan
  3. Point your phone at the QR code above
  4. Tap Confirm login in the app
ℹ️ The app will ask you to confirm login for “Home Assistant” — that's expected. This tool signs in through Home Assistant's Tuya app, so please make sure it was you who started this.
