Tuya Local Key

Log in

Where to find your user code
  1. Open the Smart Life app on your phone
  2. Tap Me in the bottom-right
  3. Tap the ⚙️ Settings gear in the top-right
  4. Tap Account and Security
  5. Scroll to the bottom and copy your User Code, then paste it above

Scan to confirm

How to scan the QR code
  1. Open the Smart Life app on your phone
  2. Tap the ➕ in the top-right, then tap Scan
  3. Point your phone at the QR code above
  4. Tap Confirm login in the app
ℹ️ The app will ask you to confirm login for “Home Assistant”. That's expected. This tool signs in through Home Assistant's Tuya app, so please make sure it was you who started this.

Waiting for you to confirm…

Loading your devices…

This can take a moment if you have a lot of devices.

Enter the subnet your Tuya devices are on, such as your IoT VLAN. Several can be separated by commas. Finds each device's local IP and protocol version by connecting to it on TCP port 6668 with its local key. Devices on another VLAN work too, as long as your firewall lets this machine reach port 6668 there. Up to 1,024 addresses. If you have many devices, the scan may take longer.

* Tuya lists this key on the gateway's sub-devices, not on the gateway.

Log out?

This removes your login, the saved device list with its local keys, and the network scan results from this app. To see them again, log in with a new QR code from the Smart Life app.
